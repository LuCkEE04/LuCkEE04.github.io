ABOUT

I'm a junior computer science major at Virginia State University, expecting to graduate in 2027. I split my time between two things I care about: writing software and helping kids fall in love with STEM.

By day — and most weekends — I develop curriculum for Summer of Discovery, a STEM enrichment program serving students ages 6 to 16. I build the lesson plans and presentations that make engineering, design, and computing click for two very different age groups. I also work part-time as a Warehouse Associate at the Food Lion Distribution Center, which keeps me grounded and disciplined about time management.

Outside of class and curriculum decks, I'm usually building something small in Python or C++: a calculator, a text-based adventure game, an early 2D platformer. I'm most interested in AI, automation, and the tools that help people learn or work more effectively.
